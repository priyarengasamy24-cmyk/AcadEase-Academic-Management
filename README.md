# 🎓 AcadEase – Smart Academic Workload Management

### Study Smart. Not Stressed.

## 📌 About the Project

AcadEase is an AI-powered academic workload management platform designed to solve the problem of academic overload and student burnout caused by unplanned deadlines and poor time management.

The platform helps students organize academic tasks, prioritize assignments, manage deadlines, and maintain a balanced study schedule.

AcadEase aims to create a smarter and stress-free academic environment through intelligent planning, workload visualization, and timely notifications.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often experience academic stress and burnout because of:
- Unplanned assignment deadlines.
- Multiple tasks and overlapping submissions.
- Poor time management.
- Uneven academic workload.
- Lack of coordination between students and faculty.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

AcadEase provides an intelligent platform that helps students manage their academic responsibilities effectively through AI-based planning, workload tracking, and smart notifications.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🤖 AI Deadline Optimizer
Intelligently prioritizes academic tasks and helps students organize their deadlines.

### 2. 📊 Workload Heatmap
Visualizes academic workload and identifies busy periods to help students balance their schedules.

### 3. 🔔 Smart Alerts
Provides timely reminders and notifications about upcoming assignments and deadlines.

### 4. 👩‍🏫 Faculty Coordination Dashboard
Helps faculty coordinate academic schedules and manage assignment deadlines.

### 5. 🧠 Mental Health Tracker
Encourages students to monitor their stress levels and maintain a healthier academic routine.

### 6. ⌚ Smart Watch Integration
Potential wearable integration for convenient reminders and student wellness tracking.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Reduce academic overload and student burnout.
- Improve time management and productivity.
- Optimize assignment deadline planning.
- Promote a balanced academic schedule.
- Improve student-faculty coordination.
- Encourage student well-being.

## 🔄 Working Process

1. Student enters academic tasks and deadlines.
2. The system organizes tasks based on priority.
3. AI Deadline Optimizer helps create a study schedule.
4. Workload Heatmap displays academic workload.
5. Smart Alerts remind students about upcoming deadlines.
6. Students track their workload and well-being.
